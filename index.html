Earth Observation · Climate · Public Health

See environmental health threats before they become health emergencies.

Sentinel HealthOS turns satellite data into risk scores for drought, flooding, heat, water stress and air quality, then tells health and disaster-management departments what action to take.

Request a demoSee how it works

From satellite pixels to departmental action

One pipeline, built so that every risk score can be traced back to the data behind it.

Observe

Earth Observation indicators: vegetation, moisture, land surface temperature, flood extent and air quality.

Combine

Weather, population exposure, healthcare access and vulnerability layered onto each indicator.

Score

A transparent risk engine produces 0–100 scores by category, municipality and province.

Recommend

Each elevated risk is paired with a suggested public-health action for the responsible department.

Six risks, one picture

Environmental conditions rarely act alone. Sentinel HealthOS shows how they stack up.

Heat

Land surface temperature and heat anomalies weighted by who is exposed and how vulnerable they are.

Drought

Vegetation stress, soil moisture and rainfall anomalies, with affected population and farmland.

Flooding

Flood extent and rainfall accumulation linked to the clinics and hospitals in harm's way.

Air quality

PM2.5 and NO₂ patterns tied to respiratory and cardiovascular risk.

Water stress

Surface water and moisture trends that signal supply problems before they hit communities.

Disease risk

Environmental signals combined with surveillance data, where legally and technically appropriate.

Built for decisions

A score is not a plan. Sentinel HealthOS recommends the action.

Dashboards show what is happening. Sentinel HealthOS goes further, matching each risk level and category to a recommended response for the department that needs to act.
